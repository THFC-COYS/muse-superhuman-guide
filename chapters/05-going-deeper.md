# Chapter 5: Going deeper

Once the basics feel natural, your agent workforce can grow. Here's what's next.

## Connectors

Muse connects to the tools you already use: Shopify, Stripe, QuickBooks, Slack, Asana, Notion, and more. Each connection is a new pair of hands. Connect them one at a time, starting with wherever you lose the most hours.

Small business owners: there's a whole playbook for this at [muse-small-biz-playbook](https://github.com/THFC-COYS/muse-small-biz-playbook).

## Gadgets

Meta open-sourced Muse Gadgets: the firmware and SDK to put Muse into physical things. A display on your desk that shows your day. A button that briefs you. Builders are sharing what they make in the community Discord.

## Custom connectors

If Muse doesn't connect to a tool you use, you can build a custom connector. You don't need to be a developer for the simple ones; the documentation walks through it.

## The principle

Add leverage one connection at a time. Every new connection should earn its place by giving you back hours or catching things you'd miss. If it doesn't, disconnect it.
