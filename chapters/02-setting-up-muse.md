# Chapter 2: Setting up Muse

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. It's free for most of what you'll need, and it lives on your phone.

## Step 1: Install it

Download Muse on iOS or Android, or open muse.ai in a browser. Sign in with your Meta account.

## Step 2: Connect one thing

Muse gets dramatically more useful when it can see your world. Start with one connection: your calendar, your email, or your Instagram. That's it. One.

## Step 3: Say hello

Tell it something real: "Here's what my week looks like. What should I be worried about?" Watch what it does with that. Most people feel the shift right here, in the first five minutes.

## What not to do

Don't try to learn everything on day one. Don't connect all your accounts at once. One connection, one task, one judgment call. You'll add more when you're hungry for it, which will be soon.
