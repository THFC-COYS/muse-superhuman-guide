# Chapter 1: The mindset shift

Here's the shift: you stop doing the work and start judging the work.

For your entire life, being capable meant doing things yourself. Writing the email. Doing the research. Making the plan. Now there's an agent that does those things in seconds, and your job becomes the part machines can't do: deciding what's good.

Agents do the work. Humans make the judgment calls.

That's not a demotion. Judgment is the most valuable skill there is, and it's the one thing AI can't fake. Anyone can generate ten options. Almost no one can reliably pick the right one. That picker is you now.

## Try this today

Give Muse one real task you'd normally do yourself. Something small: "Draft a reply to this email," "Summarize this article for me," "Plan my Saturday errands in the most efficient order." Then judge the result like a boss reviewing an employee's work. What's good? What's off? Tell it what to fix.

Congratulations. You just managed your first agent.
