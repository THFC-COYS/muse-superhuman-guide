# Chapter 3: Your first week

Five workflows. One per day. Each takes ten minutes and pays for itself immediately.

## Day 1: The inbox triage

"Go through my unread emails and give me a three-bullet summary of what actually needs me. Draft replies for the easy ones."

## Day 2: The meeting prep

"I have a meeting with [person] tomorrow about [topic]. Brief me: who they are, what they care about, and three smart questions to ask."

## Day 3: The decision memo

"I'm deciding between [A] and [B]. Here are the facts: [...]. Lay out the tradeoffs and give me your recommendation with reasons."

## Day 4: The learning sprint

"Teach me [topic] in 15 minutes. Start simple, check my understanding as we go, and don't move on until I get it."

## Day 5: The week in review

"What did I actually accomplish this week? Pull from my calendar and messages, and tell me what slipped."

## The pattern

Notice what you did each day: you gave the agent the work and kept the judgment. That's the whole game. Everything from here is just bigger versions of this week.
