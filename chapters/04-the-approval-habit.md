# Chapter 4: The approval habit

This is the one skill that keeps you in charge of your agent workforce.

Muse will not publish, send, or spend without your approval. That's the rule, and it's the feature that makes agents safe to use in real life. Your job is to build the habit of approving well.

## The three levels

**Level 1: Approve everything.** Every draft, every action, every send. Slow, but you learn what the agent gets right and where it drifts.

**Level 2: Approve by category.** Routine emails auto-send. Anything involving money, customers, or your reputation still comes to you. This is where most people should live.

**Level 3: Approve the exceptions.** The agent runs, and you only see what it flags. Fast, but only after months of level 2 earned your trust.

## The rule that never changes

Never pre-approve anything that moves money or talks to someone you care about. That approval is the job you keep. Everything else can be delegated. That can't.
